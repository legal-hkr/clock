Clock is an alarm clock, world clock, timer and stopwatch with home-screen widgets. It has no internet permission, doesn’t ask for your location, storage or media, and keeps nothing about you: alarms and settings stay on your phone.

Alarms:
- Every day, weekdays, weekends or the days you pick, or once on a date, each with its own label and sound
- Skipping the next occurrence of a repeating alarm without switching it off
- A full-screen alarm with slide to stop, snooze of the length you set, automatic silencing and gradually increasing volume
- A notification ahead of an upcoming alarm, and one for an alarm you missed
- A warning when notifications, full-screen alarms, Do Not Disturb, battery restrictions or a zero alarm volume could keep an alarm from ringing

World clock:
- Cities from the phone’s list of time zones, in the order you choose, each with its difference from local time
- A home clock that shows the time at home while you’re in another time zone

Timer and stopwatch:
- Several timers at once, each with a label, and one more minute when you need it
- Presets that start a timer with one tap, also from the home screen
- Laps with the fastest and slowest marked, ready to share

Widgets:
- Digital clock with the date and the next alarm, analog clock, world clock and timer presets

And also:
- Backup and restore of alarms, cities, timer presets and settings to a file you choose
- English and Polish, independent of the system language
- Time and date formats and the first day of the week taken from the phone’s settings
- Material 3 Expressive design with your phone’s colors
